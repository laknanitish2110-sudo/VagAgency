FAQ
Common Questions
Is this a template or custom-built?
100% custom. Every bot is built from scratch for your specific business workflow. No templates, no cookie-cutter solutions.
Do I need a WhatsApp Business API account?
Yes. We can guide you through setup (takes about 15 minutes) or set it up for you as an add-on for $50.
What about message costs?
WhatsApp Business API charges per conversation (~$0.005-$0.08 depending on country). Our bot has no per-message fees since it's self-hosted on your infrastructure.
Can the bot hand off to a human?
Yes! When the AI can't handle a query, it notifies your team via WhatsApp and email, and seamlessly passes the conversation to a human agent.
What languages does it support?
English, Hindi, Telugu, Tamil, Kannada, Malayalam, and more. The AI auto-detects the customer's language and responds accordingly.
Can you integrate with my existing tools?
Yes — Google Sheets, Airtable, HubSpot, Zoho, Stripe, Razorpay, custom APIs, databases, email, SMS. If it has an API, we can connect it.
How long does it take to build?
3-7 days depending on complexity. Starter bots ship in 3 days. Enterprise solutions with multiple workflows take up to 7 days.
Do I own the code?
Yes. You get full source code for all workflows, database schemas, and configurations. Self-hosted on your infrastructure. Zero lock-in.
